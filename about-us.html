Our Mission

The mission of CBIO Initiatives for Global Health is to expand awareness about and implementation of the Census-Based, Impact-Oriented (CBIO) Approach to working with communities to assist them in their efforts to improve their health.

Why do we need CBIO?

CBIO is a pioneering methodology to help communities improve their health. Enormous health problems still exist that communities themselves can help address. Below are causes of readily preventable death and health impairment, and the number of people throughout the world affected by them.

Undernutrition alone is linked to more than 250,000 child deaths from stunting and over one million from wasting annually, along with poorer cognitive development and lower earnings in adulthood. The number of people with vision impairment includes 826 million people with presbyopia that could be corrected with reading glasses and 94 million with operable cataracts.

Communities + Data = Lives Saved

There is much that communities can do to prevent these tragedies from occurring — both by working collaboratively with local health programs and also by local action in the community. CBIO is an approach that can help guide communities in these efforts.

Dr. Henry Perry, President

CBIO Initiatives for Global Health

Dr. Perry is a widely recognized leader in primary health care for the past 50 years. He received the Distinguished Service Award for Lifelong Dedication to Primary Health Care at the 2025 International Conference for Primary Health Care in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, “for his foundational contributions to community-based primary health care, improving equity and empowering communities worldwide.”

In 2026, the CORE Group, a global association of organizations and individuals working in community health, honored Dr. Perry by instituting an annual award named for him — the Dr. Henry Perry Global Champion in Community Health Award. He is a semi-retired faculty member in the Health Systems Program of the Department of International Health at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health.

His widely acclaimed course on Coursera, Health for All through Primary Health Care, has had more than 100,000 enrollees and runs monthly. He is the Co-Founder of Impact Global Health Alliance, the author of more than 250 publications, the most widely cited author in the domain of community health and community health workers, and, according to Clarivate, among the top 1% of authors in the field of social sciences ranked by citations for publications in the Web of Science.

Kerline T. Jules, Executive Director

CBIO Initiatives for Global Health

Kerline T. Jules has served as Executive Director of CBIO Initiatives for Global Health since 2025. She holds a bachelor’s degree in economics and a certificate in project management. She has also pursued graduate studies in organizational leadership.

With extensive experience working with various organizations in Haiti, Kerline co-founded the Rescue One Foundation in 2004 and served as its Country Director for more than 19 years. Since 2023, she has also been serving as Liaison Director for Hearts and Hands for Haiti (HHH).

Krupa Patel, DHA

Research Associate

Krupa Patel is a Research Associate with a doctorate in healthcare administration and a background spanning global health, public health, research, education, and humanitarian service.
